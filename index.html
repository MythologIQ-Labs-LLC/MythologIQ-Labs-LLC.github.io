Engineering doctrine

Evidence before confidence.

The recurring MythologIQ requirements are not decorative compliance badges. They are traceability, currentness, authority, independent evidence, exact-head CI, and explicit release or review gates.

Provenance

Claims should resolve to the source revision, evidence, policy, and validation run that produced them.

Explicit authority

Observation, recommendation, approval, execution, and durable mutation are separate capabilities.

Fail closed

Unknown, stale, unavailable, or contradictory evidence should remain visible as uncertainty rather than being converted into success.
